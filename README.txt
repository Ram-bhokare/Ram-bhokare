RAM BHOKARE - PERSONAL WEBSITE

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the ram_bhokare_website folder.
3. Double-click index.html to preview it in Chrome or another browser.

FILES
- index.html: website content and structure
- style.css: design, responsive layout and animations
- script.js: mobile menu and reveal effects
- assets/: your two supplied photographs

IMPORTANT
- This is a static starter website. Your Instagram and Snapchat profile links are included.
- Instagram posts do not automatically sync from a profile URL alone. The gallery currently displays the two photos you supplied. Automatic Instagram syncing requires an approved Instagram integration/API or another supported embed method.
- The site is not yet published online. To make it public and discoverable on Google, it must be hosted and connected to a domain (optional), then submitted for indexing.
- Review the personal details before publishing. The page shows only "23 July", not the birth year.
